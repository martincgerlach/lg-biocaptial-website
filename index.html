Approach

Responsibility

A strong sense of responsibility underpins every engagement. I provide honest, independent advice-even when it is challenging-and take ownership of the quality and impact of the work I deliver.

Long-Term Perspective

Decisions in life sciences shape value over many years. The focus is not on short-term optimization, but on building sustainable positions-scientifically, strategically, and financially.

Strategic Decision-Making

Complex science requires clear strategic choices. Lene supports leadership teams in defining priorities, evaluating trade-offs, and making decisions that stand up to investor, partner, and board scrutiny.

Capital Discipline

Capital is a critical resource. A disciplined approach ensures that financing strategies, resource allocation, and development plans are aligned to maximize long-term value creation.

Partnership Mindset

Value in life sciences is often realized through partnerships. The approach emphasizes positioning, credibility, and alignment-ensuring companies are prepared to engage with investors, pharma, and other stakeholders from a position of strength.
